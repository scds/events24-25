# Digital Inequalities: Aging and Social Connectedness in an Increasingly Online World

Join Dr. Anabel Quan-Haase as she provides an overview of the sociological literature on the challenges and opportunities of aging and social connectedness in a digital world. 

Co-hosted by the Department of Sociology and the Sherman Centre for Digital Scholarship at McMaster University.


## Facilitator Bio

Dr. Quan-Haase is a Professor and Associate Dean of Graduate and Postdoctoral, Sociology and Information and Media Studies, Western University.
