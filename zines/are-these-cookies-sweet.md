# Are These Cookies as Sweet as They Seem?

*Are These Cookies as Sweet* as They Seem? examines the topics of digital cookies, online tracking, and surveillance capitalism. I discuss how “cookies” are a metaphor that employs somewhat deceptive language to create an image of something sweet-sounding. This zine critically explores how online data collection practices are cloaked in user-friendly language and design, aiming to raise awareness and promote critical digital literacy among young internet users.
 

\- Riley Williamson
